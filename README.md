# Marketing Performance Dashboard
### Campaign Performance & Demand Generation Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes multi-channel marketing campaign data to evaluate customer acquisition efficiency, demand generation performance, and return on marketing investment. An executive-style dashboard was built to translate campaign metrics into actionable business insights.

## Business Objectives
- Evaluate campaign effectiveness across Email, Paid Search, and Social Media
- Identify high-ROI and high-cost acquisition channels
- Compare conversion efficiency and cost per acquisition
- Support data-driven budget optimization decisions

## Dataset & Metrics
The dataset represents realistic marketing campaign performance data and includes:
- Impressions, clicks, conversions
- Marketing spend and revenue
- Calculated metrics: CTR, Conversion Rate, CPA, ROI

## Dashboard Highlights
- KPI Summary: Total Spend, Total Conversions, Average CPA, Overall ROI
- Channel Performance:
  - Conversion Rate by Channel
  - CPA by Channel
- Campaign Performance Table:
  - Campaigns ranked by ROI with corresponding CPA values

## Key Insights
- Email campaigns delivered the highest ROI with the lowest acquisition cost
- Paid Search generated strong conversion volume but at a higher CPA
- Social Media campaigns showed lower efficiency and higher acquisition costs
- A small number of campaigns contributed disproportionately to overall ROI

## Tools Used
- Excel (data analysis, pivot tables, dashboarding)
- Marketing Analytics
- Data Visualization & Business Reporting

## Outcome
This project demonstrates the ability to analyze marketing data, build executive-level dashboards, and support strategic marketing decisions using data-driven insights.
